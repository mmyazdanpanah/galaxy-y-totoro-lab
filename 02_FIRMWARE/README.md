# Firmware

Historical and specimen-related firmware artifacts.

Do not place proprietary firmware binaries here until provenance, redistribution status, storage needs, and repository policy have been considered. Metadata and hashes are preferred when binaries cannot be redistributed.

## JPLC1 archive status

The locally preserved JPLC1 archive is **unverified**. Its recorded SHA-256 establishes file integrity against the locally recorded hash, but does not establish authenticity, provenance, exact device match, or safe flashing suitability.

The extracted firmware contents were removed after preservation. The source ZIP and acquisition/hash records remain local evidence for now and are not part of the repository unless separately approved.
